## 1. Product Tagline

> **“A testing and intelligence platform that measures how AI agents experience a company's digital product.”**

---

## 2. Executive Summary

AI is becoming a new discovery and interaction layer for businesses.

Customers increasingly ask AI systems questions such as:

- “What is the best product for this use case?”
- “Which service should I use?”
- “Compare these providers.”
- “Find the best option for my company.”
- “Just set this up for me.”

The AI system may search the web, retrieve documentation, compare companies, recommend a product, and increasingly take action through tools and agent interfaces.

This creates a new business problem:

**A company can have a strong product and website while AI systems still fail to discover, understand, recommend, or use it effectively.**

Our product is designed to identify that gap.

We test how AI systems experience a company's digital product, determine why the company is being ignored or why competitors are being recommended, identify the underlying causes, recommend improvements, and eventually implement and validate those improvements.

The product therefore goes beyond traditional SEO or AEO monitoring.

It is an **AI discovery, agent experience, diagnosis, and optimization platform.**

---

## 3. The Problem

Traditional web analytics were designed around human visitors.

Companies measure:

- page views
- clicks
- sessions
- conversions
- search rankings
- referral traffic

But an AI-driven customer journey can look very different:

```text
User asks AI
      ↓
AI searches / retrieves information
      ↓
AI evaluates multiple companies
      ↓
AI recommends one
      ↓
User accepts recommendation
      ↓
AI may interact with the company's product
      ↓
Task is completed
```

A company may never receive a conventional search click before the customer has already decided which product to use.

Cloudflare describes this shift as moving from traditional search ranking toward being “found, read, and confidently recommended” by AI systems. Its Agent Readiness work also frames the emerging agentic web around whether sites are readable, discoverable, callable, and eventually payable. citeturn0search5turn0search0turn0search1

This creates several unanswered questions for businesses:

1. **Is AI recommending us?**
2. **For which questions are we being recommended?**
3. **Which competitors are winning instead?**
4. **Why are they winning?**
5. **Can AI actually understand and use our website?**
6. **Can agents discover our APIs, SDKs, MCP servers, or other agent interfaces?**
7. **What technical or content changes would improve our visibility?**
8. **Did those changes actually improve AI behavior?**

Our product exists to answer these questions with evidence.

---

## 4. Our Vision

We want to become the intelligence and optimization layer for the **AI-driven customer journey**.

Instead of asking only:

> “How does my website perform for humans and search engines?”

companies should be able to ask:

> **“How does AI discover, understand, evaluate, recommend, and use my product?”**

Our long-term vision is to make the AI experience of a company's digital product measurable in the same way that traditional web analytics made human web behavior measurable.

---

## 5. What We Do

Our product follows a continuous loop:

```text
DISCOVER
   ↓
DIAGNOSE
   ↓
OPTIMIZE
   ↓
IMPLEMENT
   ↓
VALIDATE
   ↓
MONITOR
```

### Discover

We test how different AI systems discover and evaluate the client's company and products.

We investigate relevant:

- AI queries
- answer engines
- AI assistants
- web search behavior
- citations
- recommendations
- competitor mentions
- agent interactions

### Diagnose

When the client is not recommended, we investigate why.

Potential causes may exist across multiple layers:

```text
Search ecosystem
Website & content
Technical infrastructure
Agent accessibility
APIs & SDKs
MCP / WebMCP
Documentation
Authentication
Product information
Third-party sources
Competitor positioning
```

### Optimize

We determine what changes are likely to improve:

- AI discoverability
- content comprehension
- recommendation likelihood
- citation visibility
- agent task success
- interaction efficiency

### Implement

The long-term product can provide implementation services or guided changes.

Examples may include:

- content improvements
- documentation restructuring
- machine-readable content
- Markdown representations
- robots/sitemap improvements
- structured metadata
- API documentation improvements
- MCP improvements
- WebMCP integration
- Agent Skills
- authentication/discovery improvements

### Validate

We repeat the same experiments after changes.

The goal is not to claim that a recommendation “should” improve.

The goal is to measure:

> **Did AI behavior actually improve?**

## 6. Our Core Product Question

The product should ultimately answer one high-value commercial question:

> **“Why is AI recommending my competitor instead of my company, and what should I change?”**

A second question follows:

> **“After we changed it, did AI actually improve?”**

These two questions define the initial product direction.

## 7. What We Measure

The platform will eventually measure several related dimensions.

### AI Visibility

Whether and how frequently AI systems:

- mention the company
- recommend the company
- cite the company's content
- rank the company against competitors
- associate the company with relevant use cases

### Agent Readiness

Whether the digital product is technically prepared for AI agents.

Our initial conceptual framework follows:

```text
READABLE
   ↓
DISCOVERABLE
   ↓
CALLABLE
   ↓
PAYABLE
```

Cloudflare's Agent Readiness work provides an important industry reference for this layer, including checks around robots.txt, sitemaps, machine-readable content, AI access controls, agent interfaces, MCP discovery, OAuth discovery, and emerging agent commerce capabilities. citeturn0search0turn0search1

We will use this as a reference framework while extending it from simple technical readiness toward behavioral validation.

### Agent Experience

We measure how agents actually interact with the digital product:

- search behavior
- navigation
- page discovery
- content retrieval
- tool selection
- API calls
- MCP usage
- errors
- retries
- task completion
- latency
- interaction steps
- token/resource efficiency

### Agent Outcomes

Ultimately, we care about whether the agent can accomplish the intended task.

Examples:

- find the correct product
- understand pricing
- identify a capability
- complete an integration
- locate documentation
- execute an authorized action
- recommend the correct company

## 8. The Competitive Diagnosis

A central feature of the platform is **competitor diagnosis**.

Suppose a customer asks:

> “Why does AI recommend Competitor A instead of us?”

The platform should not simply return:

> “Competitor A has higher AI visibility.”

It should investigate the underlying causes.

For example:

```text
Competitor A
     │
     ├── More relevant third-party references
     ├── Better documentation
     ├── Easier-to-discover product information
     ├── Better structured content
     ├── More authoritative sources
     ├── Better machine-readable representation
     ├── Better agent interface
     └── Lower interaction cost
```

The platform should turn these observations into evidence-backed findings.

Potential signals:

- API

### Layer 4 — Product Experience

Potential signals:

## 10. Cloudflare's Role in Our Product Model

Cloudflare's work is highly relevant to our vision.

Cloudflare is building infrastructure and measurement capabilities for the agentic web, including Agent Readiness and AEO. Its AEO work measures whether AI assistants recommend companies, while Agent Readiness examines whether agents can discover and consume a site's resources. citeturn0search5turn0search0

We should incorporate these concepts rather than build an isolated “AI visibility score.”

However, our product aims to go further:

### Cloudflare-style question

> **“Is this website ready for agents?”**

### Our question

> **“Can agents successfully use this product, and if not, why?”**

And eventually:

> **“Which change improves that outcome, and by how much?”**

This distinction is fundamental to our positioning.

## 11. Agent Experience Is More Than AI Chat

The platform should eventually support multiple types of AI interaction.

### Consumer AI

This is an important initial focus because many customers interact with AI through:

- general AI assistants
- AI search
- browser-based AI
- research assistants
- shopping assistants
- other consumer-facing AI experiences

These represent the AI systems through which a company's actual customers may discover products.

### Coding / Development Agents

A second category includes:

- Codex
- Claude Code
- OpenCode
- other coding agents

These matter particularly for developer-facing products.

Vercel's AEO research demonstrates why coding agents deserve separate treatment: they operate inside full development environments, perform web searches as part of workflows, and expose agent-specific transcript and tool formats. Vercel's implementation uses isolated sandboxes and a normalization layer to turn different agent transcripts into unified events. citeturn0search2

We therefore treat coding-agent analytics as an important part of the product, but not as the only definition of the product.

### Two Observation Modes

The platform will support two complementary ways of measuring agent experience.

#### Mode A — External Agent Simulation

We control the execution environment:

Agent
→ Sandbox
→ Browser / Tools
→ Website / Product
→ Observation

This allows us to run controlled and reproducible experiments that measure
how external AI agents discover, evaluate, recommend, and interact with a
customer's digital product.

Mode A is the primary V1 product direction.

#### Mode B — Customer Instrumentation

Customers can optionally connect their own agent-facing infrastructure
through supported instrumentation such as SDKs, MCP instrumentation,
Connectors, or future agent integrations.

Customer Agent
→ Customer Infrastructure
→ Instrumentation
→ Our Platform

This allows us to measure real-world agent interactions occurring in
customer-controlled environments.

Mode B is a future expansion and is not part of the initial V1 implementation.

Both modes ultimately feed into the same canonical observation and
evaluation architecture.

## 12. Future MCP / SDK Intelligence

A major future direction is analyzing how agents use developer interfaces.

For example:

```text
Agent
  ↓
Discovers MCP / SDK
  ↓
Reads available capabilities
  ↓
Chooses a tool
  ↓
Constructs arguments
  ↓
Calls the tool
  ↓
Receives response
  ↓
Retries / recovers
  ↓
Completes task
```

The platform can eventually answer:

- Did the agent discover the MCP server?
- Did it understand the tools?
- Did it select the correct tool?
- Were tool descriptions sufficient?
- Were schemas clear?
- Did authentication cause friction?
- Did the agent retry?
- Did the tool response help the agent continue?
- Which agent performed best?
- What changes would improve agent success?

This will allow companies to optimize not just their website but their **agent-facing product interfaces**.

## 13. Before-and-After Optimization

A major product differentiator will be controlled experimentation.

Example:

```text
BEFORE

AI recommendation        38%
Citation rate             24%
Agent task success        51%
Average interaction steps 17
Average tokens            14,000

            ↓

OPTIMIZATION

            ↓

AFTER

AI recommendation        61%
Citation rate             48%
Agent task success        82%
Average interaction steps  9
Average tokens             7,000
```

The exact metrics will be defined by the evaluation system.

The important principle is:

> **Every major optimization should be measurable through a before/after experiment whenever possible.**

The initial target should be companies for whom AI discovery has meaningful commercial value.

Potential early segments:

- SaaS
- developer tools
- APIs
- fintech
- ecommerce
- marketplaces
- B2B software
- AI infrastructure

The first ideal customer should have:

1. competitors that AI can recommend instead;
2. a meaningful online presence;
3. enough product complexity for agent interaction to matter;

## 18. What We Are Not Building

To maintain focus, the initial product is **not** intended to be:

- another generic SEO platform;
- another website analytics dashboard;
- a generic chatbot;
- an AI agent builder;
- a replacement for Cloudflare;
- a generic observability platform;
- a search engine;
- an autonomous marketing agency.

Our differentiation is the combination of:

**AI discovery measurement + agent experience testing + root-cause diagnosis + optimization + validation.**

---

## 19. Product Differentiation

The product should differentiate itself through four principles.

### 1. Evidence over scores

A score alone is not useful.

Every important finding should eventually have supporting evidence.

### 2. Behavior over compliance

Technical readiness is important, but a passing technical check does not prove that agents can successfully use the product.

### 3. Competitor-aware diagnosis

We need to understand not only why the client performs poorly, but why another company performs better.

### 4. Measurement after optimization

The product should close the loop by testing whether changes actually improve outcomes.

---

## 20. Long-Term Vision

The long-term goal is to become the **measurement and optimization infrastructure for the AI-driven Internet**.

As AI becomes a major interface between customers and businesses, companies will need to understand:

```text
Can AI find us?
      ↓
Can AI understand us?
      ↓
Will AI recommend us?
      ↓
Can AI use our product?
      ↓
Can AI complete the task?
      ↓
Can the transaction happen?
      ↓
Did the experience produce the desired business outcome?
```

Our platform aims to make this entire journey measurable.

The ultimate product is not simply an AEO dashboard.

It is a system that helps companies understand and improve their **AI-facing digital product**.
